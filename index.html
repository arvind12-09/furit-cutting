Privacy Policy

Fruit Slicer 3D Blast · Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Fruit Cutting (“the Game”), developed by Arvind Kumar (“we”, “us”), handles information. We respect your privacy, and the Game is designed to work without collecting your personal data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal information. The Game does not require an account or sign-in, and it has no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves the following only on your own device so that you can continue playing:

  • Best scores and game statistics
  • Coins, unlocked blades and backgrounds
  • Mission progress
  • Your sound and vibration settings

This data never leaves your device through the Game and is deleted when you uninstall the Game. If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Android may back up this data to your own Google account. That backup is managed by Google and is not accessible to us.

3. Permissions

  • Vibration: used only for haptic feedback while playing. You can turn it off in Settings.

The Game does not access your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, or location.

4. Google Play services

The Game uses Google Play to check whether a newer version of the Game is available and to install updates. This is handled by Google Play on your device and is subject to the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive any personal information from this process.

5. Links and sharing

The Settings screen contains links to this Privacy Policy, the Google Play page of the Game (to rate it), a share option, and an email option. These open your browser, Google Play, or another app on your device. Any information you choose to send (for example, an email to us) is handled by the app you use and is used only to reply to you.

6. Children’s privacy

The Game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, no chat, no social features, and no in-app purchases with real money.

7. Data security

Because the Game does not transmit your personal data, there is no personal data on our servers to protect. Game data on your device is protected by your phone’s own security.

8. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You can erase all locally stored Game data at any time by clearing the Game’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the Game. If you have any questions or requests, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

If the Game changes in the future (for example, by adding ads, online features, or purchases), we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date above before those changes are released.
